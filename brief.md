## Overview

A national emergency response network must deliver reliable communication, accurate information, and timely coordination during critical incidents. Software protocols enable secure and predictable communication between clients and servers, while artificial intelligence can analyze traffic patterns and help anticipate congestion. Mobile applications allow field responders to access essential information, even when connectivity is temporarily unavailable, provided that offline data is synchronized carefully when service returns. Beneath these applications, 5G network slicing can help separate critical emergency traffic from ordinary traffic and support different quality-of-service requirements. Cybersecurity processes help teams detect, contain, and recover from incidents that threaten network availability or data integrity. Finally, a suitable data architecture organizes operational information for consistent reporting and analysis. These six areas must work together to support resilient emergency services.
